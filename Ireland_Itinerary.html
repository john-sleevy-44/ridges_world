Practical Notes

Driving

Drive on the left. Connemara roads are narrow — go slow and use passing places. Free parking in Clifden; Galway has multi-storey car parks near Eyre Square. In Dublin, park at your hotel — city-centre driving isn't worth it.

Weather

The west coast can do four seasons in a day. Waterproof jacket and layers are essential for Diamond Hill, Croagh Patrick, and the Cliffs of Moher regardless of the forecast. Croagh Patrick's upper cone and the cliff path can both be very exposed and windy.

Don't Miss

Connemara marble jewellery (green stone found only here), Galway oysters on the quays, a proper Irish brown bread breakfast, and a spontaneous pint in a pub that has trad music spilling out the door.
